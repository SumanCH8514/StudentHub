Frequently Asked Questions

Common Inquiries

How are class timetables synchronized?
Class schedules are updated directly by authorized faculty and departmental administrators. Changes reflect in real time on the student dashboard.
Can I use StudentHub without an active internet connection?
Yes. StudentHub utilizes Progressive Web App (PWA) caching so you can access your saved class schedules and holiday calendars even when offline.
How does attendance tracking calculate eligibility?
The system benchmarks logged sessions against the standard 75% minimum university threshold, warning students when they risk falling below examination eligibility.
